# MASTER STORY BIBLE — PART 2
# S1E06 through S1E10 (Series 1: MOHANDAS)

### S1E06 — THE WATER AND THE TAX

**Purpose:** The Kandel Canal Satyagraha in full detail — the injustice, the resistance, the leadership of Chhotelal Srivastava, the alignment with the Non-Cooperation Movement. The episode that makes Gandhi's arrival inevitable.

**Source Chapter:** Gandhian Independence Movement — The Kandel Canal Satyagraha section

**Slide 1 — Hero Card:**
Visual: Kandel village — flooded paddy fields, canal water flowing freely, a British order nailed to a tree, farmers standing firm.
Text: *"When the non-cooperation movement started in India, under its influence, farmers in the Dhamtari region of Chhattisgarh protested against the tax imposed on the canal water of the British."*

**Cold Open (Slides 2–7):**
- Slide 2: Dhamtari district. Kandel village. 70 km from Raipur. August rains.
- Slide 3: The British irrigation department accuses Kandel farmers of stealing canal water. In a monsoon. When fields have enough rain.
- Slide 4: The charge is false. The farmers know it. The British know it. The tax is the point.
- Slide 5: Policy of repression: cattle seized during farming season. Rs 4,030 confiscated.
- Slide 6: Babu Chhotelal Srivastava steps forward. A man, not a myth. He simply says: *no.*
- Slide 7: Pt. Sundarlal Sharma and Narayanrao Meghawale join the Satyagraha.

**Narrative Arc (Slides 8–33):**
The Kandel Satyagraha as a model of disciplined non-violence — a preview of the national movement's methods. The parallel to Champaran: Rajkumar Shukla bringing Gandhi there, the British forming a committee as soon as Gandhi arrived. Both sides understand the same logic — Gandhi's presence alone is the pressure. The decision: someone must go to Calcutta and bring him.

**Key Moment:**
*"The government's irrigation department accused the farmers of Kandel of stealing water from the canal. It was the month of August; it was raining heavily. There was enough water in the fields. Therefore, the charge of farmers stealing water from the canal was false."*

**Climax (Slides 34–35):**
Pt. Sundarlal Sharma sets out for Calcutta on December 2, 1920. He finds Gandhi on the eve of his journey to Nagpur. He tells Gandhi about Kandel. Gandhi agrees to come.

**Resolution (Slide 36):**
Before Gandhi even reaches Raipur, the British concede. The anti-farmer order is withdrawn. *"The British accepted the demands of Kandel Satyagrahis even before Mahatma Gandhi reached Raipur."*

**Cliffhanger (Slide 37):**
*"But Gandhi is still coming. Episode 7: The Invitation."*

### S1E07 — THE INVITATION

**Purpose:** The mechanics of how Gandhi came to Chhattisgarh. The role of Pt. Sundarlal Sharma as the bridge between national and local movement. The Khilafat connection — Ali brothers. The preparations in Raipur. The world holds its breath.

**Source Chapter:** 1920: Mahatma Gandhi's First Visit to Chhattisgarh — preparatory sections

**Slide 1 — Hero Card:**
Visual: Pt. Sundarlal Sharma speaking to Gandhi in a train compartment. Two profiles in lamplight. The train moving through night.
Text: *"Pt. Sharma informed Gandhi about the Kandel Satyagraha and accompanied him to Raipur."*

**Cold Open (Slides 2–7):**
- Slide 2: Calcutta, December 2, 1920. A city buzzing with pre-Nagpur Congress energy.
- Slide 3: Gandhi is en route from Calcutta to Nagpur for the historic session.
- Slide 4: Sharma boards the same train. A meeting on the rails.
- Slide 5: Sharma describes Kandel. Gandhi listens. The Champaran parallel is clear.
- Slide 6: The Ali brothers — Shaukat Ali and Muhammad Ali — are also travelling with Gandhi. The Khilafat movement and the Non-Cooperation movement, travelling together.
- Slide 7: Gandhi agrees to stop at Raipur. A small detour that will echo for a century.

**Narrative Arc (Slides 8–33):**
The Khilafat movement's role in Chhattisgarh — Muslim zamindars sacrificing land and Malguzari for the movement. On December 21, Shaukat Ali and Mohammad Ali address the Muslim gathering at Yateemkhana in Dhamtari. The preparation in Raipur — Pt. Ravi Shankar Shukla, Thakur Pyarelal Singh, Sakharam Dubey waiting on the platform. Gandhi lodged at Barrister Thakkar's bungalow. The Tilak Swaraj Fund collections — cash, gold, silver jewelry from everyone.

**Key Dialogue:**
Shaukat Ali said: *"I am like an elephant and Gandhi is like a goat"* — the crowd laughed. Then: *"Even if I die, it will not be of any use, but Gandhi is still useful for everyone, and will remain useful even after death."* Then people understood the context of the elephant and the goat.

**Climax (Slides 34–35):**
The evening meeting at what will become Gandhi Chowk. *"Gandhi addressed a huge general meeting."* Fifteen thousand people. The Tilak Fund fills with jewelry and coin.

**Resolution (Slide 36):**
The next morning, December 21 — Abbas Bhai's motor waits at Azad Chowk. Gandhi is about to go to Dhamtari.

**Cliffhanger (Slide 37):**
*"Tomorrow the crowd will not be able to hold itself. Episode 8: Mohandas Arrives."*

### S1E08 — MOHANDAS ARRIVES

**Purpose:** December 20, 1920 — Gandhi's arrival at Raipur. The platform moment, the third-class compartment. The evening general meeting. The living experience of encountering Gandhi for the first time. The spirit of the day.

**Source Chapter:** 1920: Mahatma Gandhi's First Visit — December 20 section

**Slide 1 — Hero Card:**
Visual: Raipur railway platform, 1920. A sea of people. A small figure in dhoti and shawl stepping from a third-class compartment, hands folded in namaste as the train begins to move.
Text: *"Seeing Gandhi going in the third class, the public got emotional after realising his simplicity and people became convinced that only Gandhi can lead the poor and downtrodden Indian people properly."*

**Cold Open (Slides 2–7):**
- Slide 2: Morning of December 20, 1920. Raipur station. Every leader from the district is on the platform.
- Slide 3: Dadasaheb Khaparde, Dr. Munje — first class and second class compartments. No Gandhi.
- Slide 4: *"Someone told that he was sitting in the third-class compartment."* The crowd runs to that side.
- Slide 5: Gandhi standing at the door of the third-class compartment, wearing Lungi and Bandage Kurta, hands folded. The train is moving.
- Slide 6: *"There was only one thing on the face of the people around"* — realisation. This is no ordinary leader.
- Slide 7: Gandhi is lodged at Barrister Thakkar's bungalow. The Ali brothers with him.

**Narrative Arc (Slides 8–33):**
The evening meeting at the ground near Kankalipara — what becomes Gandhi Chowk. 15,000 present. Gandhi speaks: the Tilak Fund, the Non-Cooperation Movement, the need for Satyagraha. The women's gathering at Machilabad in Tatyapara — a large number of Muslim women openly donating to the Tilak Fund. Gandhi visits the Jaitasava monastery. The Raipur District Congress Committee forms on the same day — Ravi Shankar Shukla its first secretary.

**Key Dialogue (Prabhu Lal Kabra, eyewitness):**
*"I saw Gandhi on the platform of Raipur, in the same postal train, Dadasaheb Khaparde, Dr. Munje, etc. were going to Calcutta... Gandhi was not visible in them and the eyes of the crowd were looking for Gandhi... Gandhi, wearing a Lungi and Bandage Kurta, was standing at the door of the third-class compartment with folded hands, till then the train started moving."*

**Climax (Slides 34–35):**
The Raipur District Congress Committee is born. A structure of resistance, permanent and organised, where none existed formally before.

**Resolution (Slide 36):**
Night falls on Raipur. Tomorrow: Dhamtari. The farmers of Kandel are waiting.

**Cliffhanger (Slide 37):**
*"Makai Chowk cannot hold what is coming. Episode 9: The Day in Dhamtari."*

### S1E09 — THE DAY IN DHAMTARI

**Purpose:** December 21, 1920 — the Dhamtari visit. The full drama of the day: the impossible crowd at Makai Chowk, Omar Seth lifting Gandhi on his shoulder, the hour-long speech, the women's gathering, the return to Raipur, and the final public meeting.

**Source Chapter:** 1920: Mahatma Gandhi's First Visit — December 21 section

**Slide 1 — Hero Card:**
Visual: Omar Seth — a broad-shouldered merchant from Gurur — lifting Gandhi onto his shoulders through a sea of people at Makai Chowk. Gandhi's hand raised in greeting, a sea of faces below.
Text: *"At that time, Omar Seth, a businessman from Gurur, lifted Gandhi on his shoulder and took him to the stage."*

**Cold Open (Slides 2–7):**
- Slide 2: Dawn. December 21, 1920. Abbas Bhai's motor waits at Azad Chowk.
- Slide 3: On receiving information about Gandhi's arrival, irrigation officials withdraw the anti-farmer order. The Satyagraha has already won. But Gandhi still comes.
- Slide 4: The road to Dhamtari fills with people. A nation on foot.
- Slide 5: Makai Chowk. The enclosure of Seth Hussain. The motor cannot reach the stage through the crowd.
- Slide 6: Omar Seth lifts Gandhi on his shoulder. The crowd parts slightly. Gandhi smiles.
- Slide 7: Bajirao Kridatta presents a bag of Rs 501 to Gandhi.

**Narrative Arc (Slides 8–33):**
Gandhi's hour-long speech at Dhamtari — praising Chhotelal Babu, Narayan Rao Meghawale, and Pt. Sundarlal Sharma. Highlighting non-violence as the foundation of Satyagraha. More than 500 activists from Dhamtari commit to attend the Nagpur session. Return journey. The massive public meeting in Kankalipara — ground next to Anand Samaj library. A drama (*Bhishma Pitamah*) had played on this stage the night before. After the meeting, those with reverence for Gandhi take bricks from the stage as mementos — the stage itself dismantled out of devotion. The women's gathering at Machilabad — open donations. The Ali brothers on Khilafat. Gandhi at the Jaitasava math.

**Key Moment — Abbas Bhai:**
He drove Gandhi to Dhamtari when the bus driver was stopped by police. Days later: *"The license of his transport company Raipur Bus Service was cancelled."* The cost of proximity to Gandhi — ordinary people paying for ordinary acts.

**Climax (Slides 34–35):**
The Raipur women's gathering. A large number of Muslim women attend. They donate openly. Hindu and Muslim women standing together for Tilak Fund.
*"Gandhi had elaborated on the outline of the Non-Cooperation Movement in these meetings."*

**Resolution (Slide 36):**
Gandhi returns to Raipur. December 22 — he departs for Nagpur by train. But the Congress Committee is formed. The lawyers will leave their practices. Titles will be surrendered. The non-cooperation movement begins.

**Cliffhanger (Slide 37):**
*"The goat and the elephant. The man and the myth. What did his leaving leave behind? Episode 10: The Spark That Stayed."*

### S1E10 — THE SPARK THAT STAYED

**Purpose:** The aftermath of Gandhi's first visit. The Non-Cooperation Movement in Chhattisgarh. Titles surrendered, lawyers leaving advocacy, national schools established, the Jungle Satyagrahas beginning. Bridge to 1933. Close of Series 1.

**Source Chapters:** Impact of the Non-Cooperation Movement in Chhattisgarh; Nagari-Sihawa Jungle Satyagraha 1922; Jhanda Satyagraha 1923

**Slide 1 — Hero Card:**
Visual: A charkha spinning alone in a lamplit room. Thread becoming a tricolor. The sound of the wheel.
Text: *"With the arrival of Gandhi, the movement for national independence reached every village, street-by-street, in Chhattisgarh."*

**Cold Open (Slides 2–7):**
- Slide 2: December 26, 1920. Nagpur Congress session. The Non-Cooperation resolution passes.
- Slide 3: Vamanrao Lakhe surrenders his Raisahab title. The people of Raipur give him the title of *Popular* on the same day.
- Slide 4: Thakur Pyarelal Singh and Ramdayal Tiwari leave the law. Lawyers become Satyagrahis.
- Slide 5: Barrister Thakkar boycotts the assembly elections he was standing for.
- Slide 6: National Schools established — Madhav Rao Sapre in Raipur, Chhotelal Srivastava in Dhamtari, Badrinath Saw in Bilaspur.
- Slide 7: Prabhu Lal Kabra and Ratnakar Jha take out a huge procession to boycott foreign goods. August 1, 1921.

**Narrative Arc (Slides 8–33):**
The Nagari-Sihawa Jungle Satyagraha of January 1922 — tribals enter the reserved forest in protest of the Forest Act. 39 arrested, none apologise to the British. Sentences of 3 to 5 months. Leaders return from Ahmedabad Congress to find a Satyagraha already happening. Pt. Sundarlal Sharma arrives, explains that AIC approval wasn't obtained, movement postponed. But: forest department agrees to minimum wages, forced labour stopped. Small victory through organised dignity. The Jhanda Satyagraha 1923 — Pt. Sundarlal Sharma arrested, sentenced to 6 months, for hoisting the national flag. Chauri Chaura 1922: Gandhi withdraws Non-Cooperation. The Swaraj Dal formed in response. Chhattisgarh continues quietly.

**Key Dialogue:**
Mohammad Yunus, told to pay tax or go to jail, said to the judge: *"I am involved in Mahatma Gandhi's non-cooperation movement from now on."* He went directly from court to jail. His seventeen-village zamindari, hundreds of acres, cattle, and gold — all confiscated.

**Climax (Slides 34–35):**
Five leaders arrested in 1922 and imprisoned in Raipur Jail: Pt. Sundarlal Sharma, Narayan Rao Meghawale, Maulana Rauf, Maulana Hamid Ali, Bhagwati Prasad Mishra. In jail they remain unbroken.

**Resolution (Slide 36):**
Series 1 closing meditation: *"Gandhi came to Chhattisgarh once as Mohandas. He was in the third-class compartment. He collected fund for Tilak. He went to a women's gathering. He stayed three days. And nothing was ever the same."*

**Cliffhanger / Series Bridge:**
*"Thirteen years will pass. He will return — not as Mohandas, but as The Mahatma. Series 2: THE MAHATMA — coming now."*
