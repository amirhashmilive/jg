Privacy Policy

This privacy policy outlines how data is handled when you visit the Johar Gandhi website. Our priority is to provide a seamless, immersive storytelling experience while fully respecting your privacy.

Local Storage Only

This website utilizes standard browser local storage exclusively to enhance your experience. We store minor state data—such as your last viewed episode and slide, playback speed, and initial visit timestamp—to allow seamless resuming of the cinematic presentation if you leave and return later.

No Tracking or Cookies

We do not use tracking cookies. We do not use third-party analytics services (such as Google Analytics). We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data, usage habits, or identifying information to any external servers.

Hosting

This static website is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit the site, GitHub may collect standard server logs (such as IP addresses) necessary for providing their hosting services, in accordance with the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Contact

For any inquiries regarding this project or its privacy practices, please contact Amir Hashmi or the Meer Foundation.
