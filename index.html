"Future generations will find it difficult to believe that such a man — made of flesh and blood — ever came to earth."

— Albert Einstein, on Mahatma Gandhi

Mahatma Gandhi visited Chhattisgarh only twice — once as Mohandas in 1920, and once as the Mahatma in 1933. Yet his presence ignited a fire that burned through every village, every forest, every heart in the region.

This is not a textbook. This is a cinematic journey through the untold stories of freedom fighters, the courage of tribal revolts, and the quiet revolution that Gandhi sparked in the land of 36 forts.
